01. Overview & Core Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Novy VPN ("we", "our", or "the App") respects and protects the personal privacy of users ("you"). We operate under a strict, verifiable Zero-Logs Policy.

We believe that internet access should be private, secure, and unrestricted. We have architected our technology specifically so that we do not possess any knowledge of your online activities while connected to our VPN tunnels.

02. Android VpnService Disclosure (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy regarding the use of Android's VpnService:

  • Core Functionality: Novy VPN utilizes the Android VpnService API to establish a device-level encrypted virtual private network tunnel. This is the primary, core purpose of our application.
  • Traffic Encryption: All outgoing and incoming network packets routed through the VPN tunnel are encrypted using high-grade cryptography protocols (such as WireGuard® and OpenVPN with 256-bit encryption).
  • No Traffic Inspection: The VpnService tunnel in Novy VPN does not intercept, modify, inspect, or sell your personal traffic, user data, or sensitive network transmissions.
  • No Data Harvesting: We do not redirect user traffic for monetization purposes or unauthorized data analytics.

03. Information We NEVER Collect (No-Logs Policy)

When you use Novy VPN, our systems are intentionally designed never to retain or log:

✕ No browsing history or visited websites
✕ No DNS queries or search queries
✕ No original user IP address logs
✕ No session timestamps tied to your identity
✕ No personal identity or account logins
✕ No transmitted file content or payloads

04. Transient Operational Data (Device-Local Only)

To provide functional VPN connection diagnostics, the application processes the following strictly transient, non-identifying data on your device:

  • Real-time Session Bandwidth: The app counts the total bytes uploaded and downloaded during your current session (via Android's TrafficStats) to display your live speed and data transfer stats. This data is never sent to any remote server and resets immediately upon disconnecting.
  • Server Latency (Ping): Real-time latency checks against public relay nodes to help you select the fastest node.

05. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep Novy VPN 100% free and maintain public VPN servers without subscription fees, we integrate third-party ad networks:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): We use Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect non-sensitive device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID - GAID) and approximate location to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • VPNGate / Public Node Relays: Server connectivity may utilize public relay endpoints hosted by academic volunteer networks (e.g. University of Tsukuba VPNGate project) under open academic terms.

06. Android Permissions We Require

Novy VPN only requests permissions strictly required for the core VPN functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: To establish remote server network sockets.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To detect Wi-Fi / Cellular changes and handle auto-reconnects.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: To keep the encrypted VPN tunnel alive when the app is in the background without Android OS abruptly terminating it.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display a persistent, non-intrusive status notification showing that your connection is protected and providing a 1-tap "Disconnect" button.

07. Children's Privacy
